Privacy Policy

KKV Ortho Clinic · Nanganallur, Chennai

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Avanthi Intelligence supports clinic operations for KKV Ortho Clinic (“the Clinic”). It describes how information may be handled when authorised clinic staff use the Avanthi Intelligence dashboard to manage patients, appointments, follow-ups and related clinic activities.

1. Information we handle

Depending on what the Clinic records in the system, information may include patient names, telephone numbers, appointment dates and times, appointment status, and operational notes or follow-up details entered by authorised staff. Please do not enter information that is not needed for clinic operations.

2. How information is used

Information is used to support the Clinic’s administrative and operational activities, such as maintaining patient and appointment records, coordinating visits, managing follow-ups, and helping authorised staff communicate with patients. The system is intended for clinic administration, not for providing emergency services or replacing professional medical advice.

3. WhatsApp messages and consent

If a patient has provided the required consent and the Clinic has recorded that preference, the Clinic may use the WhatsApp Business Platform to send service-related messages such as appointment confirmations, reminders, rescheduling notices and cancellations. Patients can ask the Clinic to stop WhatsApp messages at any time. The Clinic should honour opt-out requests and keep consent records up to date.

WhatsApp messages are processed through Meta’s WhatsApp services. Use of WhatsApp is also subject to the applicable WhatsApp and Meta privacy terms. Do not send sensitive medical details through WhatsApp unless the Clinic has assessed that the channel is appropriate and has a lawful basis to do so.

4. When information may be shared

Information may be accessible to authorised Clinic personnel who need it for their work. It may also be processed by technology providers that support hosting, database operations, or WhatsApp delivery, to the extent needed to provide those services. The Clinic does not use this platform to sell patient personal information.

5. Storage and security

Clinic records are maintained in the application’s hosted systems. Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards should be used to protect access, including limiting dashboard access to authorised personnel and keeping account credentials and access tokens confidential. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Retention

Information is retained for as long as it is needed for the Clinic’s operational purposes and applicable record-keeping or legal requirements. Retention and deletion decisions are managed by the Clinic in accordance with its obligations.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

You may contact the Clinic to ask about personal information recorded in its systems, request correction of inaccurate details, or ask about deletion or stopping WhatsApp communications. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate clinic record-keeping. The Clinic may need to verify your identity before responding.

8. Children’s information

Where the Clinic records information about a child, the parent or legal guardian should provide the information and any required consent on the child’s behalf, in accordance with applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the platform or the Clinic’s practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
